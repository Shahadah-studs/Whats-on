100% Serverless • Peer-to-Peer • Open Source

Messaging Without Middleman Servers

What'sOn connects your browser directly to your friends using WebRTC and PeerJS. Your messages, photos, and video calls travel straight from device to device—never stored in a corporate cloud database.

🔒

True P2P Encryption

Every chat tunnel and video stream is encrypted end-to-end via WebRTC DTLS/SRTP. Only you and the person you connect with can read or see what is shared.

💾

Local-First Storage

Your username and Saved DMs stay right inside your own browser's local storage. Deleting a DM wipes it cleanly from your device immediately.

📹

Consent-First Calls

Incoming calls always ask you to Accept or Decline first—with pre-call Camera and Mic switches so your lens never turns on by surprise.

🌊

Wavers (Group Rooms)

Create or join multi-person P2P rooms called Wavers to chat and hang out with an entire crew using a single room code. This feature is coming soon!

Quick Safety & Connection Tips

  • Keep your username tab open: Since there is no central server, friends can only reach you while your What'sOn tab is open and online.
  • Strict Wi-Fi or Cellular: Some school or office firewalls block direct P2P connections. Switching networks or using a TURN relay resolves it.
  • Browser Data: Clearing your browser site data resets your saved DMs and releases your claimed username.
